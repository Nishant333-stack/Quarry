# Billing and Invoicing

## Payment terms

Invoices are issued on the day a shipment is picked up. Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Enterprise-tier customers may negotiate net 45 terms in their master services agreement.

## Late payment

An invoice becomes overdue on the day after its due date. Overdue balances accrue a late fee of 1.5% per month. Accounts with any invoice more than 60 days overdue are placed on credit hold, and new shipments must be prepaid until the balance is cleared.

## Disputing an invoice

A customer may dispute an invoice within 21 days of the invoice date. The dispute must identify the invoice number and the specific charge in question. While a dispute is open, the disputed amount does not accrue late fees. Meridian responds to billing disputes within 5 business days.

## Credit notes

Credits for late deliveries and approved claims are issued as credit notes against the original invoice. An invoice whose full amount has been credited is marked as credited. A credit note can never exceed the amount of the invoice it is applied to.

## Accepted payment methods

Meridian accepts bank transfer, ACH and corporate card. Card payments above USD 5,000 carry a 2.9% processing surcharge.
