# Damage and Loss Claims Policy

Meridian Freight compensates customers when a shipment is damaged or lost while in our custody. This policy explains who can claim, the deadlines, and how the payout is calculated.

## Filing deadlines

A damage claim must be filed within 14 days of the delivery date. Claims filed after 14 days are denied unless the customer can show the damage was concealed and could not reasonably have been found earlier, in which case the window extends to 21 days.

A shipment is treated as lost when it has no tracking scan for 10 days after its promised delivery date. A loss claim must be filed within 60 days of the ship date.

## Evidence required

Every damage claim needs photographs of the damaged goods, photographs of the outer packaging including the shipping label, and the commercial value of the goods (an invoice or receipt). Claims without packaging photographs are returned to the customer once for completion before being denied.

## Liability limits

Meridian's liability is limited to USD 25 per kilogram of shipment weight, and never more than the invoice amount of the shipment. For example, a 12 kg shipment invoiced at USD 480 has a maximum payout of USD 300, because 12 x 25 = 300 is lower than the invoice amount.

Customers who bought declared-value coverage are instead covered up to the declared value, with a ceiling of USD 50,000 per shipment.

## Exclusions

Claims are denied when the packaging did not meet the Packaging Standards, when the goods were prohibited items, or when the damage was caused by a customs inspection.

## Payout timeline

Approved claims are paid within 10 business days. Enterprise-tier customers are paid within 5 business days. Payouts are issued as a credit on the customer's account unless a bank refund is requested.
