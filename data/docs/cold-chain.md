# Temperature-Controlled Shipping

## Temperature bands

Meridian's cold chain service offers three bands: frozen at -20 C or below, chilled at 2 to 8 C, and controlled ambient at 15 to 25 C.

## Monitoring

Each temperature-controlled shipment travels with a data logger that records temperature every 10 minutes. The log is available in the customer portal within 2 hours of delivery.

## Excursions

A temperature excursion is any reading outside the booked band for more than 30 consecutive minutes. When an excursion is detected, Meridian alerts the customer within 1 hour and holds the shipment for the customer's disposition instruction.

## Claims for temperature damage

Claims for goods spoiled by a temperature excursion follow the Damage and Loss Claims Policy, with two differences: the data logger report replaces the photograph requirement, and the claim must be filed within 7 days of delivery.

## Packaging

Shippers using their own passive packaging must qualify it for at least 48 hours longer than the planned transit time. Meridian's active containers are available for pallet-sized loads and must be booked 3 business days ahead.

## Availability

Cold chain service is available on Express and Standard service levels only.
