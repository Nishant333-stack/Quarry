# Customer Tiers

Meridian Freight has three customer tiers. The tier is set by annual shipping spend and reviewed every January.

## Tier thresholds

| Tier | Annual spend | Support response target |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Under USD 50,000 | 8 business hours |
| Priority | USD 50,000 to 250,000 | 2 business hours |
| Enterprise | Over USD 250,000 | 30 minutes, 24/7 |

## Standard tier

Standard customers use the self-service portal and email support. Claims are paid within 10 business days.

## Priority tier

Priority customers get phone support, a 5% discount on Standard service, and one free redelivery attempt per shipment.

## Enterprise tier

Enterprise customers have a named account manager, a quarterly business review, net 45 payment terms on request, and claim payouts within 5 business days. Late delivery credits for Enterprise customers are applied automatically without a request, provided the delay is not excluded from the guarantee.

## Tier changes

A customer whose spend falls below the threshold keeps the higher tier for a 90-day grace period before being moved down.
