# Customs Documentation for International Shipments

## Required documents

Every international shipment needs a commercial invoice and a packing list. The commercial invoice must show the seller, the buyer, a description of each item, its quantity, unit value, currency, country of origin and the Harmonized System (HS) code.

Shipments valued above USD 2,500 leaving the United States also need an Electronic Export Information (EEI) filing.

## HS codes

The HS code determines the duty rate. Meridian requires at least the first 6 digits of the HS code for each line item. A shipment with missing or invalid HS codes is held at origin and is not tendered to the carrier until corrected.

## Customs holds

When customs authorities hold a shipment, Meridian notifies the customer within 4 business hours. The customer has 5 business days to supply missing documents. After 5 business days, storage is charged at USD 35 per day. A shipment still on hold after 20 business days is returned to the sender at the customer's cost.

Delays caused by a customs hold are excluded from the on-time delivery guarantee.

## Duties and taxes

By default, duties and taxes are billed to the recipient (Delivered At Place). Customers can choose Delivered Duty Paid, in which case Meridian pays the duties and bills them to the shipper with a 2% disbursement fee, minimum USD 15.
