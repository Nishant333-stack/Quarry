# Dangerous Goods

## What Meridian accepts

Meridian accepts the following classes of dangerous goods on Standard and Economy ground services only: Class 3 flammable liquids in limited quantities, Class 8 corrosives in limited quantities, and Class 9 miscellaneous goods including lithium batteries.

Dangerous goods are never accepted on Express service.

## Lithium batteries

Lithium-ion batteries packed with or contained in equipment are accepted when each battery is rated at 100 watt-hours or less. Standalone lithium-ion batteries must be shipped at a state of charge of no more than 30%. Damaged, defective or recalled batteries are prohibited.

Each package containing lithium batteries must carry the lithium battery mark, and the shipper must declare the battery type and watt-hour rating at booking.

## Prohibited items

Meridian does not carry explosives (Class 1), toxic gases (Division 2.3), infectious substances (Division 6.2) or radioactive material (Class 7) under any circumstances.

## Surcharge and penalties

A dangerous goods handling surcharge of USD 45 applies per shipment. Undeclared dangerous goods discovered in transit incur a penalty of USD 500 per package, and the shipment is held until it is made compliant or collected by the shipper.
