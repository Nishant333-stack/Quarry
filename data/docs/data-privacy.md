# Data Handling and Privacy

## What we store

Meridian stores the shipper and recipient names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses needed to move a shipment, plus customs data for international shipments.

## Retention

Shipment and invoice records are retained for 7 years to meet tax and customs requirements. Proof-of-delivery signatures and photographs are retained for 18 months. Support conversations are retained for 24 months. Call recordings are retained for 90 days.

## Customer data requests

A customer may request a copy of, or deletion of, their personal data. Meridian responds to these requests within 30 days. Records that must be kept by law, such as invoices, are excluded from deletion but are restricted from further use.

## Access controls

Support agents see only the customers assigned to their region. Payment card numbers are never stored by Meridian; card payments are tokenised by the payment processor. Recipient phone numbers are masked in the driver app after delivery.

## Sharing with third parties

Personal data is shared with partner carriers and customs brokers only as needed to complete a shipment. Meridian does not sell personal data.

## Incident notification

If a data breach affects customer data, Meridian notifies affected customers within 72 hours of confirming the breach.
