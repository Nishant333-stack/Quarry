# Service Levels and On-Time Delivery Guarantee

Meridian Freight offers three service levels. Two of them carry a money-back guarantee when we deliver late.

## Service levels

| Service level | Typical transit | Guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| Express | 1-2 business days | Yes |
| Standard | 3-5 business days | Yes |
| Economy | 6-10 business days | No |

## Late delivery credits

Lateness is measured in calendar days between the promised delivery date and the actual delivery date.

For Express shipments, a delivery that is 1 or 2 days late earns a credit of 25% of the invoice amount. A delivery that is 3 or more days late earns a credit of 50% of the invoice amount.

For Standard shipments, a delivery that is 3 or more days late earns a credit of 10% of the invoice amount. Standard shipments that are 1 or 2 days late do not earn a credit.

Economy shipments have no delivery guarantee and never earn a late delivery credit.

## Requesting a credit

The customer must request the credit within 30 days of the delivery date. Credits are applied to the invoice for the late shipment and cannot exceed that invoice's amount.

## Exclusions

The guarantee does not apply when the delay was caused by a customs hold, by severe weather or another force majeure event, by an incorrect or incomplete delivery address, or when the recipient was unavailable on the first delivery attempt.
