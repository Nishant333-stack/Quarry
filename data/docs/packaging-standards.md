# Packaging Standards

Shippers are responsible for packaging that protects goods during normal handling. Claims for damage are denied when these standards were not met.

## Cartons

Cartons must be new or in like-new condition, made of double-wall corrugated board for any package over 15 kg. Each item needs at least 5 cm of cushioning on all sides. A single carton may weigh at most 30 kg; heavier goods must be palletised.

## Pallets

Pallets may be at most 120 cm by 100 cm in footprint, 180 cm in height including the pallet, and 1,000 kg in gross weight. Goods must not overhang the pallet edge. Loads must be stretch-wrapped with at least 3 layers and secured with 2 straps in each direction.

## Fragile items

Glass, ceramics and electronics must be double-boxed, with the inner box suspended in at least 7 cm of cushioning. A fragile label alone does not qualify a shipment for special handling.

## Liquids

Liquids must be in sealed containers placed inside a leak-proof liner with enough absorbent material to contain the full volume.

## Labelling

The shipping label must be on the largest face of the package. Old labels and barcodes must be removed or fully covered.
