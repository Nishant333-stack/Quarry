# Failed Delivery, Redelivery and Returns

## Delivery attempts

Meridian makes up to 3 delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After each failed attempt the recipient is notified by email and SMS with a link to reschedule.

## Holding and storage

After the third failed attempt the shipment is held at the local depot. Storage is free for 5 calendar days. From day 6, storage is charged at USD 4 per day per package.

## Return to sender

A shipment not collected or rescheduled within 15 calendar days of the third attempt is returned to the sender. The return is billed at the Economy rate for the same lane.

## Redelivery fees

A redelivery requested after the three standard attempts costs USD 18 per package. Priority-tier customers receive one free redelivery per shipment, and Enterprise-tier customers are not charged for redelivery.

## Address corrections

An address correction requested after pickup costs USD 22 and may add 1 business day to transit. A delay caused by an incorrect address is excluded from the on-time delivery guarantee.
