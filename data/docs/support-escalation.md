# Support Escalation and Approval Limits

## Priority levels

| Priority | Definition | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Shipment lost, safety incident, or enterprise outage | 30 minutes |
| P2 | Damaged shipment or delivery more than 3 days late | 2 business hours |
| P3 | Billing dispute or documentation question | 8 business hours |
| P4 | General enquiry | 2 business days |

## Approval limits for credits

Who may approve a credit depends on its amount.

A front-line support agent may approve credits up to and including USD 200. Credits above USD 200 and up to USD 1,000 need a support supervisor. Credits above USD 1,000 need approval from the finance team.

No one may approve a credit larger than the invoice it is applied to.

## When to escalate to a human

A ticket must be handed to a human supervisor when the customer threatens legal action, when a safety incident is reported, when the same shipment has two or more previous claims, or when the requested remedy is outside written policy.

## Goodwill credits

Outside the written guarantee, a supervisor may grant a goodwill credit of up to USD 100 once per customer per calendar quarter. Front-line agents cannot grant goodwill credits.
