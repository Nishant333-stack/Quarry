# Surcharges and Accessorial Fees

## Fuel surcharge

A fuel surcharge is added to every shipment as a percentage of the base freight rate. The percentage is reset on the first Monday of each month from the national average diesel price, and is published in the customer portal.

## Oversize and overweight

A package with any side longer than 150 cm incurs an oversize fee of USD 60. A package heavier than 30 kg that is not palletised incurs an overweight handling fee of USD 40.

## Residential and remote delivery

Delivery to a residential address costs USD 6 per shipment. Delivery to a remote postcode costs USD 28 per shipment; the list of remote postcodes is in the customer portal.

## Liftgate and inside delivery

Liftgate service for pallet deliveries without a loading dock costs USD 75. Inside delivery beyond the first threshold costs USD 95.

## Weekend delivery

Saturday delivery is available on Express service for USD 20 per shipment. Sunday delivery is not offered.

## Waivers

Enterprise-tier customers have residential and liftgate fees waived. No other accessorial fee is waived by tier.
